# SpatialScout: your task

Thank you for trying SpatialScout. We are testing the tool, not you. There are no wrong answers, and you can stop at any time.

**Where:** open the URL your facilitator gives you: ____________________

**Time:** about 12 minutes.

## Background

SpatialScout works on a public kidney spatial-transcriptomics dataset (GSE286184, Xenium, 50 genes). It has sections from control kidneys and from four glomerular diseases: diabetic kidney disease, IgA nephropathy, minimal change disease and membranous nephropathy. It checks whether a result still holds when the rule for assigning RNA molecules to cells gets stricter, for example counting only molecules over the nucleus.

## Your task

Ask SpatialScout about this dataset, typed or spoken, and use what it shows you to answer three questions on your rating form:

1. **One result that holds** when molecule assignment gets stricter, with the evidence label SpatialScout gives it.
2. **One result that does not hold**, or "none found" if you could not find one.
3. **Which result deserves follow-up in the lab**, and why, in a sentence.

If you are not sure what to ask, try one of these:

- "Do podocytes in this atlas really co-express LRP2 and CUBN, or is that segmentation?"
- "Which DKD differences in proximal tubule cells survive nucleus-only assignment?"

## Along the way

- Read the plan before you approve it. You can reject it.
- Open at least one claim and look at its tissue evidence.
- If something looks wrong, say so. You can also try "Challenge the verifier" on a claim card to see what the checks catch.
- Think aloud if you are comfortable doing so.

**You are done when** you have written an answer to all three questions on the rating form.

Every result is a research hypothesis from public data, not a diagnosis or a clinical recommendation.
