## Project Description

2025 / 3000 characters. Paste only the text below.

SpatialScout is a spatial-omics co-scientist powered by Meta models, built for kidney researchers investigating unexpected molecular signals in tissue.

A researcher must connect tissue images, gene and protein measurements, analysis controls and papers before choosing a follow-up experiment. SpatialScout brings that work into one delegated investigation: it plans supported analyses, runs scientific tools, requests controls, checks claims and returns inspectable evidence, figures and suggested next experiments.

Our first working module tests whether RNA signals persist when the rules assigning molecules to cells become stricter. It combines an interactive tissue viewer, random-loss and cell-label sensitivity controls, independent numerical recomputation, model-based figure review and a follow-up lab for cited literature and separate protein-image evidence. Researchers can independently inspect biopsies, draw regions for fresh analysis, and explore measured RNA molecule depths in 3D with depth slicing and quantitative profiles. Compatible Xenium sections can also be uploaded for a fresh deterministic audit.

We ingested 22 deposited kidney RNA sections (21 distinct) and provide four real KPMP protein-imaging biopsies. The RNA cohort includes diabetic kidney disease and other glomerular diseases; the protein specimens are separate healthy/CKD/AKI participants. Results are exploratory research evidence, not clinical conclusions.

The featured live-model investigation made 18 model calls, took 179 seconds and cost an estimated $0.29; its follow-up lab has separate timing and cost. The public site replays that recording, labelled as such; box, depth and sample-data analyses compute fresh, and visitors can start live runs through a capped trial or their own model keys. Human time savings have not yet been measured.

Try it: https://chandrarupdaka--spatialscout.us-east.modal.run
The report links claims to computed artifacts and exports figures, a hash-linked audit log and a verification notebook.

## Describe your use of Meta products to build the submitted project

1636 / 3000 characters. Paste only the text below.

Muse Wonder 1.0 plans the investigation, decomposes the researcher's question into testable sub-claims and dispatches follow-up lab tasks.

Muse Spark 1.3 reads analysis results and chooses controls, including random-thinning and cell-label sensitivity checks. It also reads computed figures as one part of claim verification and summarizes literature records fetched from Europe PMC.

Muse Spark Open, served through Baseten with Fireworks configured as failover, drafts claims against computed evidence. The gateway validates responses, supports repair and records model/provider usage, latency and estimated cost.

SAM 3.1 supplies box-prompted exploratory outlines on RNA-derived section renderings. These are inspectable proposals rather than fine-tuned or anatomically validated cell boundaries, and they do not determine the primary evidence labels.

Muse Voice Transcribe supports spoken research questions with an editable transcript.

These models operate through a bounded tool registry and persisted investigation workflow. Deterministic Python tools compute measurements and controls; an independent numerical path rechecks cited values. After the investigation, Wonder dispatches literature, protein-cross-check and figure tasks that run concurrently. The interface exposes outputs and provenance, while replay preserves genuine recorded calls.

Modal hosts the public app and persistent investigation storage, and ran a parallel per-section batch. We use Meta's reasoning, vision, segmentation, speech and open-weight offerings for distinct jobs rather than asking one prompt to generate an unsupported scientific answer.

## Why are Meta’s models important to your solution?

1551 / 3000 characters. Paste only the text below.

Spatial biology involves several kinds of work: planning a multi-step investigation, interpreting quantitative outputs, inspecting figures, proposing image regions and accepting a researcher's spoken question. Meta's model ecosystem gives us complementary components for those roles within one scientific harness.

Wonder handles investigation planning and specialist dispatch. Spark handles the tighter reasoning loops, literature interpretation and figure inspection. SAM adds concrete geometry, while Voice Transcribe makes task delegation accessible through speech. Spark Open provides an open-weight route for claim drafting and a path to alternative deployment and adaptation.

The important integration is that model decisions lead to real scientific tools and inspectable artifacts. A model can request a control or explain evidence, but our code computes the measurements and checks numerical citations. Source identities and spatial coordinates stay attached to the result. Large tissue images require a tiled, coordinate-aware data pipeline; selected previews and figures are used for model interpretation.

The recorded investigation demonstrates planning, agent-selected controls, claim repair and multimodal review on real kidney data. Meta models make these complementary roles practical to compose. We have not run a controlled model comparison and do not claim greater scientific accuracy than competing models. The contribution is a domain-specific workflow whose usefulness and reliability can be evaluated, reproduced and extended.
