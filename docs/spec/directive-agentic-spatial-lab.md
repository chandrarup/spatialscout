# SpatialScout: The "Agentic Spatial Lab" — Complete Step-by-Step Demo Plan, Architecture Directive & Judge Q&A Playbook

> **CORE POSITIONING FOR JUDGES & CODING AGENTS:** SpatialScout is not a static bioinformatics script—it is an **Agentic Spatial Lab for Precision Hypothesis Testing**. A researcher or translational scientist opens two real spatial `.tif` biopsies side-by-side (**Healthy Reference** vs. **Individual Patient Biopsy**), selects regions on both, and states a biological intuition/hypothesis in plain language or voice. An autonomous team of Meta AI models (`Muse Wonder`, `SAM 3.1`, `Muse Spark Open` on **Baseten**, `Muse Glimmer` on **Fireworks**, and `Muse Spark 1.3` Vision Verifier on **Modal**/Meta API) segments the selected tissue, strips out cell-boundary spill-over illusions ([Mitchel et al. 2026](https://www.rna-seqblog.com/impact-and-correction-of-segmentation-errors-in-spatial-transcriptomics/)), runs downstream spatial neighborhood analysis ([Voyager](https://pachterlab.github.io/voyager/articles/codex_landing.html)), and returns **verified, falsifiable claims (`CONFIRMED` vs. `REFUTED`)** plus an **Individual Patient Precision Research Profile** for downstream validation—generalizing seamlessly from Kidney Disease to Oncology and Neurodegeneration.

## 2. Challenging Research Hypothesis Questions to Pre-Load as 1-Click Chips in the UI

To show judges that SpatialScout is a true **Agentic Spatial Lab** capable of handling complex, multi-step biological reasoning (not a single hard-coded query), include these **4 Challenging Hypothesis Chips** right below the search/voice bar:

1. **Chip 1 — The "Hybrid Cell State vs. Neighbor Bleed" Challenge (Hero Demo Query):**
   > *"In Patient #DKD-04 vs. Healthy Control, do glomerular podocytes genuinely acquire a tubular endocytosis program (`LRP2`/`CUBN`), or is that signal lateral spill-over from adjacent proximal tubules—and does `UMOD` in infiltrating B-cells survive the same boundary audit?"*
   * **Why it's challenging:** Requires testing two candidate signals simultaneously where one is **real intracellular biology** (`LRP2`/`CUBN` in podocytes survives `nucleus_only` and label-circularity checks) and the other is a **false positive segmentation artifact** (`UMOD` in B-cells collapses by >75% once boundary halo molecules bordering thick ascending limb tubules are excluded).
2. **Chip 2 — The "Spatial Microenvironment & Fibrotic Niche" Challenge:**
   > *"Test the hypothesis that `POSTN+` myofibroblasts and infiltrating T-cells (`CD3D`/`CD8`) physically cluster within 25 µm of injured podocytes in this patient's glomeruli rather than being randomly distributed across the cortex."*
   * **Why it's challenging:** Requires combining `SAM 3.1` glomerular boundary masks with spatial neighborhood colocalization ([Pachter Lab Voyager methods](https://pachterlab.github.io/voyager/articles/codex_landing.html)) and testing whether the cell-cell proximity enrichment still holds after removing shared boundary transcripts.
3. **Chip 3 — The "Single-Patient Precision vs. Cohort Generalization" Challenge:**
   > *"Is the vascular endothelial injury signature (`VCAM1`, `PECAM1`) in this patient's selected biopsy region an isolated single-patient outlier, or is it reproducibly elevated across the 22-biopsy glomerular disease cohort under leave-one-section-out validation?"*
   * **Why it's challenging:** Bridges **individual patient biopsy inspection** with **cohort-wide statistical rigor** (`leave-one-section-out` across [GSE286184](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13586693/)), showing how a precision-medicine researcher checks if an individual patient aligns with a broader responder subgroup.
4. **Chip 4 — The "Cross-Disease Generalization" Challenge (Alzheimer's / Immuno-Oncology Preview):**
   > *"Apply the same SpatialScout boundary de-contamination audit to microglia surrounding Aβ plaques in Alzheimer's cortex (or PD-1+ T-cells bordering tumor nests): how much apparent immune-tumor co-expression is membrane segmentation bleed?"*
   * **Why it's challenging:** Demonstrates immediately to VC and startup judges that the exact same mathematical and agentic architecture applies to every spatial proteomics/transcriptomics platform (PhenoCycler/CODEX, Xenium, CosMx, Merscope) across oncology and neurology ([Mitchel et al. 2026](https://www.rna-seqblog.com/impact-and-correction-of-segmentation-errors-in-spatial-transcriptomics/)).
